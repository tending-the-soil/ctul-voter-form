🗳 Voting Eligibility & Registration

🔍 Let's check your eligibility

Minnesota has broad eligibility. You can vote if you are a U.S. citizen, at least 18 on election day, and have lived in Minnesota for 20+ days.

→ Check your eligibility on the MN Secretary of State website

→ Consulta tu elegibilidad en la página web del Secretario de Estado de Minnesota

🤝 You can still make a difference!

📝 Let's get you registered!

Minnesota allows same-day voter registration, but registering early saves time at the polls.

Online: mnvotes.org | En español - mnvotes.org

By mail: Print and mail a form from the Secretary of State

In person: Register at your polling place on Election Day — bring proof of residence

You'll need: MN ID or last 4 of SSN
